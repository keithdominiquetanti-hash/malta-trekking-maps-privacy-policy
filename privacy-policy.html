Privacy Policy for Malta Hiking Maps

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Malta Hiking Maps ("the App") collects, uses, and stores information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location Data

The App requests access to your device's precise GPS location while you are actively using a hike route. Location data is used solely to display your position on the map, detect proximity to hike checkpoints, and auto-centre the map view. Location data is processed on-device only and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

1.2 Data You Provide

The App stores the following information locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Hike progress (completed hikes, unlocked hikes, completed checkpoints)
  • Earned badges
  • Hike star ratings you submit after completing a route
  • Pledge balance amounts you choose to record voluntarily
  • App preferences and settings

None of this data is transmitted off your device or shared with any third party.

1.3 Photos

If you choose to add personal photos when sharing your hike achievements via the social sharing feature, those photos are accessed temporarily to generate a shareable image. The App does not store, upload, or retain your photos beyond the immediate sharing action. Photos are shared directly via your device's native share sheet to an app of your choice.

1.4 Contact Form Messages

If you submit a message via the in-app Contact screen, your name, email address, and message content are transmitted to the app developer via EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. This data is not used for marketing or shared with any other party. Please refer to the EmailJS Privacy Policy at https://www.emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy/ for further information on how they handle data in transit.

1.5 Hiking Route Data

The App fetches hiking route data (maps, GPX files, checkpoint information, badge images) from a remote server operated by the app developer (hosted on Cloudflare). This data is cached locally on your device for offline use. No personal information is included in these requests.

2. Data Storage and Security

All personal progress data (completed routes, badges, ratings, pledge amounts) is stored exclusively on your device using Android's built-in SharedPreferences storage. This data does not leave your device and is not accessible to the app developer or any third party.

Cached route content (maps, images, GPX files) is stored on your device using standard Android cache storage and may be cleared by the operating system or manually by you at any time.

3. Voluntary Contributions

The App includes an optional feature that allows you to voluntarily pledge and transfer a personal contribution to support the app's development. Any such transfer is made directly from your own banking or payment application to the developer's personal bank account or Revolut account using the details displayed in the app. The App itself does not process, handle, or store any payment card details or banking credentials. The pledge balance figure you choose to record is stored locally on your device only.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenStreetMap — provides map tile imagery displayed during hikes. OpenStreetMap data is fetched from public tile servers. No personal data is included in tile requests beyond your device's IP address, which is standard for any internet request.
    Privacy policy: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • EmailJS — used to deliver contact form submissions to the developer by email. Only the content you enter in the contact form is shared.
    Privacy policy: https://www.emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Cloudflare — hosts the hiking route data and media files fetched by the App. Standard request logs (including IP address) may be retained by Cloudflare per their policies.
    Privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

The App does not use advertising networks, analytics platforms, or tracking services of any kind.

5. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — required to show your position on the hike map and detect checkpoint proximity. Used only while the App is in use.
  • INTERNET — required to fetch route data, map tiles, and badge images.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — required only if you choose to add personal photos to the social sharing feature.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Since all personal data generated by your use of the App is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You may delete the App at any time to remove all locally stored data. You may also clear the App's data via your device's Settings at any time.

If you submitted a contact form message and wish to request deletion of that correspondence, please contact the developer at the email address provided in the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact
