About the App

Malta Hiking Maps is a completely free, donation-supported mobile application designed for local and international hikers exploring the Maltese archipelago. The application provides offline-ready tracking, detailed checkpoints, and digital route achievements without subscriptions or paywalls.

Core App Features & Specifications for Hikers

  • Turn-by-turn Navigation: Interactive visual map tracks for rural trails, coastal circuits, and cliffside paths.
  • Dynamic Seasonal Route Catalog: Tailored for local Mediterranean weather, featuring lush Nature Winter Hikes during the blooming season and cooler History Summer Nights walks.
  • Phased Route Rollouts: A continuously expanding library of local walking tracks.
  • Interactive Checkpoints: Built-in educational text pop-ups highlighting local history, hidden gems, and heritage landmarks.
  • Gamified Hiking Achievements: Unlockable milestone badges tracking completed routes and reached hidden gems and iconic landmarks as a token of the amazing memories.
